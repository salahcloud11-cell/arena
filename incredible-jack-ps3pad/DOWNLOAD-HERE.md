## New v4 controls

- **Cursor visibility:** Control Studio has an on-screen SHOW/HIDE switch plus **CHANGE CURSOR
  SHOW/HIDE BUTTON**, which learns any PS3 button.
- **Menu visibility:** **CHANGE MENU SHOW/HIDE BUTTON** learns any PS3 button. L3+R3 always remains
  available as emergency menu recovery.
- **Mouse click:** tap CROSS (X) to click at the cursor; it does not also fire X's mapped target.
- **Mouse scrolling:** hold CROSS (X) for about half a second, then scroll with the right stick or
  D-pad arrows. Release X to leave scrolling mode.
- **Original game buttons:** the `HIDE ORIGINAL GAME TOUCH BUTTONS` section lets you independently
  mask the movement, Cross-area, Circle-area, Square-area and Triangle-area graphics. Their touch
  regions and controller-generated touches remain functional. The masks continuously sample the
  surrounding game scene so they do not become mapper target markers.

All v3 features remain: continuous 60 Hz edge-bounded cursor movement, Smart Input, arbitrary
mappings, invisible mapper targets, 1x–5x multi-click, saved profiles and a completely hideable bar.
